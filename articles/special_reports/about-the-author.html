Summary

I was a student at SAIT until graduating with a diploma in Information Security Systems (ISS) in August 2025. I was looking for work until a year later I eventually landed my dream career starting job as a security analyst. I started learning about coding and computers at the age of twelve where I learned Python Object Oriented Programming from a book (yes a litteral book). Now I spend my time creating fun projects, working on personal skills, exploring the wilds, and developing my skillset.

Follow along the journey of how I got to be who I am today.

All Good Stories Start Somewhere

I had walked into a book shop with my Dad and was looking around. I had a list of books I was looking for in the kids section but to no avail. We were mainly there to get a Christmas present for my mom. As I was looking around I stumbled on the reference section. I flipped through a couple books until I landed on one: Idiots Guide Beginning Programming by Matt Telles. My Dad came over and I begged him to get the book for me. I made a promise to read it front to back, and to do all the programming examples it had inside. Looking back it is crazy to see how one book changed my future so drastically. If I never got this book would I be where I am today? I don't think so as this book had opened a door to a world just beneath my fingertips.


Junior High and High School (Grades 7-12) I took all available computer science courses. I was a little dissapointed with Junior High as they were just typing tests and using Scratch for coding. I felt limited on what I could do and I wanted to do more. I did SAIT summer camps where I learned to make games using Dreamweaver IDE and build websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Honeslty, this is probably where my hatred towards JavaScript started but years later I would find a language so much worse. Its distant cousin and my least favourite language (sorry) of all time Java...


High School was where my self taught programming came in handy. We started to touch actual programming languages and IDE's in a school setting. During this time I had flipped between Web, Software and Game development. Grade 10 and 11 we were using JavaScript to create browser based games. Grade 12 we were using Java to create software. My final project was essentailly Uber (this was when uber first came out around this time) and I called it OnlineCabs. It was an app that would help solve getting a cab for my city easier. Honestly it was a lot of fun and it used Java, SQL, HTML, and a payment gateway.

The Project Hellscape

Do you know what goes into a project? Well during my Junior High and High School I was working through endless slop videos on YouTube (at the time they had value to me) just working on personal fun projects. I would search for something that would keep my brain engaged. This was again before AI and I was using Youtube or programming books to learn. The old school method but they were still mostly object oriented programming. I wanted to find a way to break free from creating another knock off social media following the same video thousands of other people had watched.


After graduating High School I was doing upgradding at a local University and I took a Visual Basic course while there. During that course I helped the professor with helping students during lab time and working on a side project. I wanted to take Stack Overflow and create a software application for it. I used SQl and Visual Basic to create an app based on the premise of De-Ducking and Stack Overflow. A debugging platform that I was able to post questions, answers, seperate profiles, and it had a little duck that I was able to read my code line by line to while I thought about what it should do. The project came out to be a thousand plus lines of code. I was happy with my project and content with the amount of work I had put in.


I still continued to do more slop projects but I was starting to break free from the project hellscape. Where everyone is doing the same project following the same videos and creating content as if they were their own ideas. I think that is where my voice and want to create my own channel came from.

Where I am at Now

Well now of days I have graduated SAIT and have a diploma in Information Security Systems. I have a Youtube channel I post very infrequently to. And I am working towards getting multiple certifications to futher develop my knowledge (or getting ones I have the knowledge for already). I working on my personal growth just as much as my development skills. The beauty of getting older is you find a job to help pay to expand your growth oppotunities.
